Format notes

        
01 / Write
Start with cmp1, then legal UCI moves. Lowercase. One space between moves.
cmp1 e2e4 e7e5
02 / Move
Use source and destination squares. Add a piece letter for promotion. Castling uses the king move.
e2e4 · e7e8q · e1g1
03 / Continue
After checkmate or stalemate, the next move starts a new game. PGN separates the games.
